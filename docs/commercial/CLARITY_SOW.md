## 1. Purpose

Client engages Magnus to produce a **board-ready diagnostic** of funding concentration, ecosystem / funder landscape context, and DAF readiness signals—so leadership can see risk and opportunity clearly before committing to ongoing platform or deeper implementation work.

This is a **fixed-scope professional services** engagement. It is **not** a software subscription, not autonomous AI acting on Client’s behalf, and not a guarantee of funding outcomes.

---

## 2. Scope of work (included)

Magnus will deliver the following:

### 2.1 Intake & data collection
- Kickoff call (up to 60 minutes)
- Structured intake questionnaire
- Review of Client-provided materials (see §5 Client responsibilities)

### 2.2 Funding map & concentration risk
- Map of primary revenue / funding sources (as supported by Client data)
- Concentration metrics (e.g., largest source; top-3 share) with plain-language risk notes
- Assumptions and data gaps called out explicitly where evidence is incomplete

### 2.3 Ecosystem / funder landscape map
- High-level landscape relevant to Client’s mission and geography (not an exhaustive market study)
- Prioritized observation list for near-term attention (6–12 month horizon)

### 2.4 DAF readiness snapshot
- Baseline DAF readiness checklist (profile consistency, public discoverability cues, website language gaps)
- Key metrics Client can track going forward
- *Does not include* full Candid seal optimization unless `SVC-DAF` is purchased as an add-on

### 2.5 Recommendations & synthesis
- Prioritized recommendations for the next 6–12 months
- Synthesis call / walkthrough (up to 60 minutes)
- Final deliverable package (PDF and/or slide deck as agreed at kickoff)

Light implementation support after delivery is a **change order**, not part of this SOW.
